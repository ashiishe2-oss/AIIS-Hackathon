Operational Workflow

How AIIS Protects Healthcare Capacity

A multi-layered intelligence platform for citizens and public health authorities.

🔍

1. Intelligent Discovery

Citizens can instantly locate public healthcare facilities based on precise proximity, specialties, and infrastructure tiers without navigating fragmented state databases.

⚡

2. Telemetry & Early Warning

The backend synthesizes bed occupancy rates, doctor-to-patient staffing ratios, and OPD growth velocities into a unified Strain Risk Index (SRI) to forecast overload 7–14 days in advance.

🔄

3. Coordinated Mutual Aid

District health officers can redirect non-emergency patient surges from strained tertiary centers to low-risk secondary facilities before capacity reaches critical saturation.

Platform Capabilities

Advanced Public Health Engineering

Built for real-world public health infrastructure across India.

📈

Predictive Strain Forecasting

Random Forest machine learning algorithms evaluate seasonal disease spikes, weather factors, and OPD surge trajectories to identify impending capacity bottlenecks.

🗺️

Spatial GIS Risk Mapping

Real-time interactive geographical heatmaps visualize facility-level strain across entire districts, enabling tactical ambulance routing and inter-facility triage.

🛡️

Government Authority Command

Role-based security dashboards provide District Health Officers (DHO) and Hospital Superintendents with instant mutual aid directives, resource planning, and incident escalation.
