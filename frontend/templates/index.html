About the Platform

Built for the People
Who Protect Public Health

AIIS – Advanced Integrated Intelligence System is a state-level AI platform engineered to detect emerging health threats 7 to 14 days before they escalate into full-scale outbreaks. By continuously analyzing hospital telemetry, disease surveillance feeds, and population mobility data, AIIS generates actionable risk scores for every monitored facility in the district.

The platform serves district health officers, hospital administrators, and state epidemiology teams — giving decision-makers a unified operational picture. Whether allocating surge capacity, deploying medical personnel, or coordinating inter-hospital transfers, AIIS ensures every action is evidence-based and timely.

In a landscape where hours matter, AIIS replaces reactive crisis management with precision-led, proactive public health governance — protecting communities before the strain becomes visible.
